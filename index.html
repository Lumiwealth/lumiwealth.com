We work with investment firms, property managers and lenders. Our founder ran fintech at a commercial real estate lender.

Property management

Coordinators buried in maintenance requests?

Requests arrive by portal, email and phone. Someone sorts each one, sends it to the right superintendent or vendor, and chases it. Work orders get closed before the job is really done, and residents are not told when it is.

How we help

We set AI up to sort every request and draft the reply, chase open work orders, and tell residents when a job is finished. Your coordinators review and approve. As the portfolio grows, the team keeps up without burning out.

Real estate lending

Still retyping deal packages?

Rent rolls, statements and appraisals arrive as PDFs and get keyed in by hand. Every credit memo starts from a blank page.

How we help: We teach your team to pull the numbers out with AI, draft the first memo, and check covenants against the statements. An analyst still reviews every number.

Family offices and asset managers

Reading more than you're deciding?

Manager updates, capital calls, quarterly letters and client notes pile up faster than a small team can read them. Hedge funds and advisors feel it too.

How we help: We set AI up to summarize, file and draft the first version, inside your compliance rules. Your team edits and sends.

Private equity

Diligence eating your weekends?

CIMs, data rooms and portfolio reports. Hours of reading before any real thinking starts.

How we help: We train deal teams to read, compare and summarize documents with AI and get to a first draft of the memo faster. Nothing leaves your approved tools.

Also banks, credit unions and M&A advisory. If your team works with money or buildings, we should talk.
