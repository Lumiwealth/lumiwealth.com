Teaching finance and AI since 2019.

Lumiwealth started as a school. For seven years it has taught people who work with money, from analysts to portfolio managers, to use code and AI on their own work. More than 14,000 people have come through.

Now we bring that same teaching to the firms those people work for: family offices, asset managers, private equity, property managers and lenders. In your office, on your systems, one person at a time.
